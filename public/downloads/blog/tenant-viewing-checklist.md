# Tenant Viewing Checklist for Zimbabwe

Use this before you pay a deposit or sign a lease.

## Before you travel

- Confirm the exact suburb, nearest landmark, and viewing time.
- Ask who will show the property: owner, agent, caretaker, or relative.
- Ask what is included in the rent: ZESA, water, WiFi, levies, refuse, security, parking.
- Ask the move-in date, deposit, lease period, and notice period.
- Ask for current photos or a short walkthrough video.

## At the viewing

- Test taps, lights, sockets, locks, geyser, toilets, drains, doors, and windows.
- Check for damp, roof marks, exposed wiring, cracks, leaks, and broken fittings.
- Confirm water setup: municipal supply, borehole, tank, pump, or water schedule.
- Confirm power setup: prepaid meter, solar backup, inverter, generator, or shared meter.
- Check phone signal and internet options inside the property.
- Look at parking, gate, boundary wall, lighting, and general security.

## Before paying

- Confirm who has authority to receive money.
- Ask for written lease terms or at least a written offer.
- Pay through a traceable method where possible.
- Keep receipts, screenshots, messages, ID details shared with you, and viewing photos.
- Record existing defects before moving in.

## Red flags

- You are pressured to pay before viewing.
- The person refuses to show authority to rent the property.
- Bank or mobile money details keep changing.
- The rent is far below similar properties without a clear reason.
- The person avoids written terms.
